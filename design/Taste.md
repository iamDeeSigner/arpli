# Taste · Arpli
Read before generating directions, prototypes, screens or copy. Starts from `~/design-os/taste/base-taste.md` (low confidence, drawn from saved inspiration) and the Arpli brand docs in `docs/brand/` (written for this project).

**For v1:** screens are built on the current design system and kept plain while we work out how things work. This file mainly guides the Polish & QA stage. Voice rules apply from day one, because words are part of how things work.

## Feel in three words
Steady, warm, grounded (from `docs/brand/tone-of-voice.md`; the fourth pillar is "enabling"). Guiding phrase: "the knowledgeable friend who's been where you're going."

## References (why each one)
- None collected yet. Pattern research happens in Explore (Mobbin, Eagle).

## Do
- Make the person the author: "here's your draft", never "I generated" (tone of voice, Enabling)
- Be specific and concrete; use the person's own words back to them (tone of voice, Warm)
- Make empty states invite a specific next step (tone of voice, empty states)
- Use one accent colour on warm neutrals; design dark mode as a first-class mode (colour system)
- Keep motion calm and bounded: no overshoot, reduced motion designed first (motion language)
- Base: clear hierarchy, generous spacing, real content and real states (base taste)

## Don't
- Hype words: "AI-powered", "unlock", "leverage", "seamless", "supercharge" (tone of voice, banned list)
- Emoji in system copy, exclamation marks outside real celebrations, "oops" (tone of voice)
- Pure white or pure black, gradients, many accent colours, colour-coding by feature (colour system)
- Confetti, bounce, spinning spinners, pulsing dots (motion language)
- Browser `alert()` and `confirm()` pop-ups (found in the current code; off-system)

## Type, colour, motion
- Now (v1 build): whatever the current design system provides (see DesignSystem.md)
- Polish target: Inter + Fraunces (display, rare) + JetBrains Mono · warm paper, warm ink, amber primary · 120 / 220 / 360 ms, cubic-out (`docs/brand/`)

## Voice
- We are a calm, knowledgeable friend, not a hype tool or a ghostwriter.
- Words we use: draft, shape, sharpen, tighten, your story, your words, one question left.
- Words we avoid: see the banned list in `docs/brand/tone-of-voice.md`.
